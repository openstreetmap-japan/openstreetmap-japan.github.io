# This Week in Mappy Hour Japan (November 15–21, 2026)

This article is based on posts from Mappy Hour on the OpenStreetMap Japan Discord server.

Every Saturday night, Mappy Hour is held on the OpenStreetMap Japan Discord server — a relaxed online get-together where everyone maps together. This week (November 15–21), 12 people took part and there were 46 posts.

Seven people gathered for the main Saturday-night session (November 21, 21:00–24:00). The detailed map of Umekita no Mori, created at the Umekita Datathon, was showcased, and work progressed on fixing building shapes east of Yamato Station, adding intersection names around Mojiko, and filling in roadside commercial areas in Obihiro and Otofuke.

Over the course of the week, the station building and plaza of Kohoku Station were redrawn, coverage of Chuo City advanced (220 of 280 points, with the former Tatomi Town area fully covered), forests on Mukaishima were drawn, and shop information around Musashimurayama and Tachikawa was corrected. Work has also begun on reviewing the OSM data used in Dragon Quest Walk, fixing shops that have closed and correcting name errors.

<figure>
  <img src="/images/current/mappyhour-20261120-kohoku.jpg" alt="The redrawn Kohoku Station building and plaza">
  <figcaption>The redrawn Kohoku Station building and plaza</figcaption>
</figure>

The topic of the week was how to tag the burned-down North and South Halls of Shuri Castle. A participant who had visited the site raised the question, and after looking into lifecycle prefixes, the tagging was corrected to `destroyed:building=yes`. Other topics included how to tag "Seisen Ichiba TOP" and whether to propose it as an NSI (Name Suggestion Index) candidate, and the load on Overpass servers.
